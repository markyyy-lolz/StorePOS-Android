# StorePOS v1.5.0 — Retail Operations Suite

StorePOS v1.5.0 is a major Android retail-operations update focused on faster cashier workflows, stronger inventory control, safer staff access and dedicated POS-terminal features.

## POS & Scanning
- Hardware and camera barcode scans auto-add exact barcode/SKU matches.
- Successful scans provide beep + vibration feedback.
- Unknown barcodes can be created directly from the POS and immediately added to the cart.
- External customer display support mirrors the live cart and total to an HDMI / presentation display.

## Training Mode
- Owner/Admin/Manager can enable device-local Training Mode.
- Practice checkouts never post real sales, payments, inventory movements or reports.
- PayMongo and production posting are blocked while training.
- Completed training transactions are clearly marked and cannot print as real receipts.

## Inventory
- Full Android Category Manager synced with StorePOS Cloud.
- Assign categories during Add/Edit Product.
- Rename, describe, enable/disable and reorder categories.
- Stocktake scanner mode increments physical counts by barcode/SKU scan.
- StorePOS retail product inserts now default to item_type=product.

## Retail Operations Workspace
- Retail Suite, Retail Control and Register Operations are consolidated into one Retail Ops workspace.
- Existing returns/refunds, voids, cash drawer, shifts, X/Z reports, reconciliation, approvals, supplier purchasing, transfers, promos, reservations and retail health tools remain available.

## Staff & Devices
- Owner/Admin Staff & Permissions center with role assignment and active/inactive staff control.
- Role summaries clarify access for Admin, Manager, Cashier, Inventory and Mechanic.
- Device Management lists registered StorePOS terminals, app version, last-seen time and assigned user.
- Devices can be revoked to require license reactivation.

## Notifications
- Notification Center now includes All, Critical and Warning filters.
- Existing Android alert notifications continue to surface important StorePOS issues.

## Compatibility
- Android 8.0+ (minSdk 26)
- Package: com.storepos.app
- Version code: 17
- Version name: 1.5.0
- Existing shops, licenses, products, sales and StorePOS Cloud data are preserved.
